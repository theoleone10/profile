No. 01

Kaeru

A JLPT grammar trainer that fits into one daily routine.

Year2026
RoleSolo: design, code, content
PlatformiOS (Expo / React Native)
StackTypeScript, SQLite, FSRS, Supabase, Jest
● Private repo · App Store soon
KaeruJLPT N5 → N1

The idea

Each JLPT level is a train line and each grammar point is a stop. The Today screen gives you one plan to work through: learn a couple of new points, review what’s due, do your Anki flashcards, then reach your immersion time goal. It works fully offline. Signing in is optional and only backs your progress up to the cloud.

What I built

  • FSRS spaced-repetition scheduling over a local SQLite database, with migrations and versioned backups
  • Cloud sync through Supabase (Apple, Google or email sign-in) that shares one format with the backup file
  • Typed-answer reviews: romaji turns into kana as you type, and a correct but different answer gets “Close!” instead of a fail
  • Anki .apkg import that renders your own decks, plus a built-in study timer
  • Grammar content for N5–N1, checked by tests. The whole Jest suite runs in four time zones
No. 02

Paperboy

Your newsletters, turned into one short audio brief every morning.

Year2025 – 2026
RoleSolo: app, backend, infra
PlatformiOS + Node API
StackExpo, TypeScript, Express, Supabase, Gmail API, OpenAI, Inworld TTS
GitHub ↗ Site ↗
PaperboyRecorded on device, Oct 2026

The idea

You sign in with Google and choose which newsletters you want. At the time you set, Paperboy reads the latest issues and writes one script at the length you picked. It records the script in the voice you chose and sends a notification when your episode is ready. Gmail access is read-only, and only the newsletters you chose are ever read.

What I built

  • Gmail sync and parsing to find newsletters and pull out the readable text
  • A pipeline from summary to podcast script with GPT-5 mini, with control over episode length
  • Text-to-speech with Inworld, private audio storage in Supabase, and push notifications
  • Security work before App Store review: signed JWT sessions kept in the keychain, ownership checks, signed OAuth state, rate limits and in-app account deletion
  • A React Native player with a chapter for each newsletter, a full transcript, an archive of past briefs, background audio and a Live Activity
No. 03

Waypass

The whole cremation workflow, from intake to completion, on one platform.

Year2026
TypeMulti-tenant SaaS
StackReact 19, Node, Express, Supabase, PostGIS, Tailwind
GitHub ↗

The idea

Funeral homes still run cremations with phone calls and paper. Waypass connects them with crematoriums and shipping partners, follows each case from intake to completion, and gives families their own view of a case’s progress.

What’s in it

  • Case pipeline covering intake, custody, documents and completion
  • Booking flow: the funeral home proposes times, the crematorium responds, the home confirms
  • PostGIS search for nearby partners, shown on Google Maps
  • Family portal, financial dashboard, in-app inbox and Resend email
  • Workspaces with roles, and a Cmd+K command palette that searches everything
