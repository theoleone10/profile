About

Who I am

I'm an Applied Mathematics & Computer Engineering student at Queen's University, somewhere at the overlap of rigorous theory and systems that actually have to work.

My coursework covers algebraic structures, distributed algorithms, and low-level systems programming, but honestly, most of what I've learned has come from building things that I'm interested in and have real-world impact. I try to write code that's clean and correct, the kind you can easily reason with when something's on fire.

When I'm not in class, I'm usually building something: automating a workflow that was wasting my time, making a tool I actually wanted to exist, or going deeper on distributed systems just because the problems are interesting. I'm drawn to health-tech and developer tooling, areas where well-made software can genuinely change how people work or live, not just move numbers around.

I'm currently looking for internship and co-op roles. I want to work on hard problems alongside people who take their craft seriously.
